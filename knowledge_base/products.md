# Products and Sizing

## What shoe brands do you carry?

We carry a wide selection of premium and popular brands including Nike, Adidas, Puma, New Balance, Converse, Vans, Dr. Martens, Timberland, Clarks, and many others. Our catalog includes over 5,000 products.

## How do I find my shoe size?

Use our online size finder on the product page or download our sizing guide. Measure your foot in centimeters or inches and compare it to our size chart. Different brands may have slightly different sizing, so always check the specific brand's size guide.

## Do you carry sizes outside the standard range?

Yes, we carry extended sizes including children's sizes (starting at size 1), women's sizes up to size 14, and men's sizes up to size 16. Specialty sizes may have limited availability.

## How do I know what size to order if I'm between sizes?

We recommend ordering the larger size. Shoes can feel tight initially, but they typically loosen with wear. If they're too large, you can easily exchange them for a smaller size.

## Do shoes come with insoles?

Most shoes come with basic insoles. Some premium brands include cushioned or removable insoles. Check the product description for details on insole type and materials.

## Can I order custom or personalized shoes?

We don't offer personalization or custom orders at this time. However, many brands we carry offer customization directly through their websites.

## What materials are the shoes made from?

Our shoes use various materials including leather, suede, canvas, mesh, synthetic materials, and rubber. Check the product description and materials section for specific details.

## Are your shoes vegan or eco-friendly?

We carry a selection of vegan and eco-friendly shoes. Use our filter options to search for vegan, sustainable, or environmentally friendly products. Check individual product pages for certification details.

## Do you have orthopedic or medical shoes?

Yes, we carry supportive shoes suitable for people with orthopedic needs. Categories include arch support, wide fitting, and specialty medical shoes. Consult your podiatrist for specific recommendations.

## Are your shoes waterproof?

Some styles are waterproof or water-resistant. Check the product description for waterproofing information. Most casual shoes are not fully waterproof but may resist light moisture.

## What is the shoe break-in period?

Break-in time varies by brand and style, typically 1-2 weeks of regular wear. Some shoes are more comfortable immediately, while others take longer. Quality leather shoes often improve with break-in.

## Do you offer half-size shoes?

Yes, many brands offer half-sizes. Check the "Available Sizes" section for each product to see if your size is in stock.

## What is the weight limit for shoes?

Our shoes don't have weight restrictions. They're designed for typical adult wear. If you need specialized shoes for specific weight considerations, contact our support team.

## Do you have shoes for specific sports?

Yes, we carry specialized shoes for running, basketball, soccer, hiking, tennis, and other sports. Browse our sports category or search for specific sport types.

## Are your running shoes suitable for marathons?

We carry marathon-grade running shoes from leading brands. Choose shoes with proper cushioning, support, and breathability. Check product reviews from other marathon runners for recommendations.

## Do you carry children's shoes?

Yes, we have a full children's section with sizes starting from toddler up to youth sizes. Children's shoes are designed for different needs including growth room and appropriate support.

## How often do you restock popular items?

We restock weekly. Popular items are restocked frequently, but availability depends on brand supply. Subscribe to product alerts to be notified when items are back in stock.

## Can I purchase shoes in bulk for my team or organization?

Yes, we offer bulk discounts for orders of 10 or more pairs. Contact our sales team at bulk@shoesstore.com for custom quotes.

## Do you carry discontinued styles?

We occasionally have discontinued or limited-edition items in clearance. These are available while stock lasts and cannot be reordered once sold out.

## What is your return policy for clearance items?

Clearance items are fully returnable within 30 days using our standard return policy. No additional restrictions apply to sale or clearance products.

## Are product photos accurate to actual color?

We use professional photography to represent our products accurately. Colors may vary slightly due to lighting and screen settings. Read customer reviews for color descriptions from actual buyers.

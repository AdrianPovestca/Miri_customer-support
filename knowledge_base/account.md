# Account Management

## How do I create an account?

You can create an account by visiting our website and clicking the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner. Enter your email address, create a strong password, and fill in your basic information. You'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account.

## Can I change my email address?

Yes, you can update your email address in your account settings. Go to your profile, select "Account Settings," and click on "Change Email." You'll need to verify your new email address before the change takes effect.

## How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address, and we'll send you a password reset link. Follow the link in the email and create a new password. The link expires after 24 hours for security.

## What should I do if I can't log in?

First, try resetting your password using the "Forgot Password" link. If you still can't access your account, ensure you're using the correct email address. Contact our support team if the issue persists.

## Can I have multiple accounts?

We allow one account per email address. If you need to manage multiple accounts, use different email addresses for each one.

## How do I update my profile information?

Log in to your account and go to "Profile Settings." You can update your name, phone number, address, and other personal information. Click "Save Changes" when finished.

## Is my account information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your account. Your password is never stored in plain text, and your personal information is never shared with third parties.

## How can I delete my account?

You can request account deletion from your account settings. Click on "Delete Account" and follow the confirmation steps. Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Can I set up a business account?

We currently offer standard personal accounts. Business customers can use personal accounts with business email addresses and delivery addresses.

## What is two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Two-factor authentication adds an extra security layer to your account. After entering your password, you'll need to verify your identity using a code from your phone. You can enable this in your security settings.

## How do I link my social media account?

You can link your Google, Facebook, or Apple account for faster login. Go to "Connected Accounts" in your settings and follow the authorization steps.

## Can I change my account username?

Your email address serves as your username and account identifier. It cannot be changed directly, but you can update your display name in your profile settings.

## What languages does the platform support?

We support English, Spanish, French, German, and Chinese. You can change your language preference in your account settings.

## How long is my session active?

Your session remains active for 30 days of inactivity. For security purposes, sessions will automatically log out if inactive for more than 30 days.

## Can I export my account data?

Yes, you can request a copy of your account data. Go to "Privacy Settings" and select "Download My Data." We'll prepare a file with all your information and email it to you.

# Technical Support

## What browsers do you support?

We support Chrome (latest version), Firefox (latest version), Safari (latest version), and Edge (latest version). For best experience, keep your browser updated to the latest version.

## Is the website mobile-friendly?

Yes, our website is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices. You can browse, search, and checkout seamlessly on phones and tablets using iOS or Android.

## Can I use the website on a tablet?

Yes, the website works perfectly on tablets. Use your tablet's browser to access our site, or download our mobile app for an optimized experience.

## Do you have a mobile app?

Yes, we have mobile apps for iOS and Android available in the App Store and Google Play. The app includes additional features like barcode scanning and push notifications.

## Why is the website slow for me?

Slow performance can be caused by poor internet connection, browser cache, or outdated browser. Try clearing your browser cache, closing other tabs, and ensuring your internet connection is stable.

## What should I do if the website won't load?

Try refreshing the page. If that doesn't work, clear your browser cache and cookies. Try a different browser or device. If issues persist, contact support as it may be a server issue.

## Why can't I add items to my cart?

Ensure you've selected both a size and color. Some items may be out of stock. Refresh the page and try again. If the problem persists, try a different browser or device.

## Can I save items to view later?

Yes, click the heart icon on any product to add it to your "Favorites." Your favorites are saved to your account and visible in your profile.

## Why did I get logged out unexpectedly?

Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity for security. You can re-login at any time. Check the "Remember Me" box to stay logged in longer on your device.

## Is there an autocomplete feature for search?

Yes, our search includes autocomplete suggestions as you type. The suggestions show popular searches and exact product matches.

## How do I search for shoes by color?

Use our advanced search filters. Select "Color" and choose from available options like Black, White, Red, Blue, etc. You can select multiple colors to see all variations.

## How do I search for shoes by price range?

Use the price filter slider to set your minimum and maximum budget. You can see results within your price range in real-time.

## Can I sort results by customer reviews?

Yes, you can sort by highest rated, most reviewed, or see reviews from verified buyers. Filter results by rating to see only highly-rated products.

## What should I do if I experience a checkout error?

Try refreshing the page and starting checkout again. Clear your browser cache. Try a different payment method. If errors continue, contact support with details about the error message.

## Why is my password not being accepted?

Ensure Caps Lock is off and you're entering the correct password. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you can't remember your password, use the "Forgot Password" link to reset it.

## What should I do if I see a security warning?

Our site uses SSL encryption and is secure. If you see warnings, it may be a browser issue. Ensure your browser is updated. If warnings persist, contact support.

## Can I disable cookies on your website?

Our website requires cookies for basic functionality like shopping cart and login. Disabling cookies may cause issues. We recommend keeping cookies enabled for our site.

## Is your website accessible to people with disabilities?

Yes, we comply with WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards. We support screen readers, keyboard navigation, and high contrast modes. Report any accessibility issues to support@shoesstore.com.

## Why can't I see product images?

Check your internet connection and try refreshing the page. Disable browser extensions that might block images. Clear cache and cookies. Try a different browser if images still don't appear.

## Can I use the website without JavaScript?

Some features require JavaScript, but basic browsing and search work without it. However, checkout requires JavaScript. Enable JavaScript for the best experience.

## Why am I getting error pages?

Error pages usually indicate temporary server issues or problems with your connection. Refresh the page and try again. If errors persist, contact support.

## Can I report a bug or issue?

Yes, contact support@shoesstore.com with details about the issue, your browser, and steps to reproduce it. We appreciate feedback to help us improve.

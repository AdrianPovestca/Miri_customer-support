# Returns and Exchanges

## What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy from the date of purchase. Shoes must be unworn, unsoiled, and in original condition with all tags attached. Worn shoes showing signs of use cannot be returned.

## How do I initiate a return?

Log into your account, go to "My Orders," find the order you want to return, and click "Return Item." Follow the prompts to select the items and reason for return. You'll receive a prepaid return shipping label via email.

## Do I have to pay for return shipping?

No, we provide prepaid return shipping labels for all returns within our standard 30-day period. Simply print the label and drop off your package at any carrier location.

## How long does the refund process take?

Once we receive your return, we inspect it and process the refund within 5-7 business days. The refund is credited back to your original payment method. Please allow 3-5 additional business days for your bank to process the refund.

## Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?

Yes, you can request an exchange for a different size or color. Simply select "Exchange" instead of "Return" when initiating your return. There are no additional exchange fees.

## What is the condition requirement for returns?

Shoes must be unworn and unused with all original packaging and tags. They should be in the same condition as when received. Worn shoes, even if lightly worn, do not qualify for returns.

## Can I return shoes that were on sale?

Yes, sale items are returnable within 30 days as long as they meet our return condition requirements. Refunds are issued at the sale price you paid.

## What if the shoes don't fit?

If the shoes don't fit, you have 30 days to return or exchange them for a different size. We offer free exchanges for sizing issues.

## Can I return items after 30 days?

Our standard return window is 30 days. After this period, returns are not accepted except in cases of defective products, which can be claimed within 90 days of purchase.

## How do I know if my return was received?

You can track your return shipment using the tracking number provided on your return label. Once received, we'll send you an email confirmation.

## What if I receive a damaged or defective shoe?

Contact us immediately with photos of the damage. We'll arrange a free return and either ship a replacement or issue a full refund, regardless of the return window.

## Can I return items bought at a discount?

Yes, discounted items are returnable, and the refund is based on the amount you paid, not the original price.

## Is there a restocking fee for returns?

No, we don't charge restocking fees. Your refund is the full amount you paid minus any applicable shipping costs.

## How do I return an order with multiple items?

You can return individual items or the entire order. When you initiate a return, select which items to return. You'll receive one prepaid return label to send all items together.

## What if I lost the return label?

Log into your account and request a new return label. We'll email it to you immediately.

## Can I return items from a gift?

Yes, gifts can be returned within 30 days. Returns are issued as account credit or refunded to the original payment method used.

## What about final sale items?

Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. Check product descriptions carefully before purchasing.

## Can I do a return or exchange after receiving a refund?

No, once a refund is processed, the item is yours to keep. Returns can only be initiated before the refund is completed.

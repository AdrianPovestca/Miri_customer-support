# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is the best way to contact customer support?

You can reach us through email at support@shoesstore.com, live chat on our website (Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST), or by phone at 1-800-SHOES-NOW. Email responses typically come within 24 hours.

## Do you have physical store locations?

Currently, we operate as an online-only retailer. We don't have physical retail stores, but we're exploring pop-up shops in major cities.

## What is your customer service availability?

Email support is available 24/7. Live chat and phone support are available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST, and Saturday-Sunday, 10am-4pm EST. We're closed on major holidays.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes, we offer complimentary gift wrapping for orders. Select the gift wrapping option during checkout, and add a personalized gift message if desired.

## Can I send a gift directly to someone?

Yes, during checkout, you can enter the recipient's address and add a gift message. We'll wrap the gift and ship it directly to them without including invoice or price information.

## Do you have a loyalty program?

Yes, we have the ShoeRewards program. Members earn points on every purchase and can redeem them for discounts. Sign up for free on our website.

## How do I earn ShoeRewards points?

You earn 1 point for every dollar spent. Bonus points are awarded during promotional events and for referrals. Points are automatically added to your account.

## How do I redeem ShoeRewards points?

Once you've accumulated points, you can use them as a discount on future purchases. Select "Redeem Points" during checkout and choose how many points to use.

## What is the referral program?

We offer rewards for referring friends. When a friend makes their first purchase using your referral link, you both get $10 credit toward future orders.

## How do I sign up for email updates?

Visit our website and enter your email in the "Subscribe" field at the bottom of the page. You can also manage subscription preferences in your account settings.

## Do you send promotional emails?

Yes, subscribers receive exclusive offers, new product announcements, and sale notifications. You can unsubscribe or manage preferences anytime in your account.

## How often do you have sales?

We have seasonal sales throughout the year including Spring Sale (April), Summer Clearance (August), Black Friday (November), and End-of-Year Sale (December). Subscribe to updates for sale announcements.

## What is your Black Friday sale like?

Black Friday typically features 20-50% off select items, plus additional discounts for members. The sale runs from Thursday to Monday. Popular items sell out quickly.

## Do you offer student discounts?

Yes, verified students receive a 10% discount on all purchases. Verify your student status through our partner verification service during checkout.

## Do you offer military discounts?

Yes, active and retired military receive a 15% discount. Verify your military status using our partner verification service.

## Do you have senior discounts?

Yes, seniors (65+) receive a 10% discount. You'll need to verify your age at checkout using your ZIP code and date of birth.

## Are there first-time customer discounts?

Yes, first-time customers receive 15% off their first order using code WELCOME15 at checkout.

## How do I report a problem with my order?

Contact support@shoesstore.com or call 1-800-SHOES-NOW with your order number and detailed description of the issue. Attach photos if applicable.

## What should I do if I'm unsure about my shoe size?

Use our online size finder or download our sizing guide. If still unsure, order the size you typically wear plus request a free exchange if it doesn't fit.

## Is there a price match guarantee?

We don't offer price matching, but we adjust prices regularly. If the price drops within 7 days, contact support for a partial refund of the difference.

## Can I cancel my order after placing it?

If your order hasn't shipped, you may be able to cancel it. Contact support immediately with your order number. Cancellations are processed within 24 hours if possible.

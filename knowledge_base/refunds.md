# Refunds and Credits

## How do I get a refund?

Initiate a return through your account in "My Orders." Once we receive and inspect your returned shoes, we'll process your refund. The refund is credited to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after receiving the return.

## Why hasn't my refund been processed yet?

Refunds typically take 5-7 business days after we receive your return. If you returned an item more than 7 days ago, check your account for refund status or contact support.

## How do I track my refund status?

Log into your account and check the return status in "My Orders." You'll see when we received your return and when the refund was issued. You can also contact support with your order number for detailed information.

## Why is my refund taking longer than expected?

Refunds may take longer during high-volume periods or if there's an issue with your return. We'll email you if there's a problem. Contact support if your refund hasn't arrived after 10 business days.

## Will I get the same amount back that I paid?

You'll receive a refund for the product price and applicable taxes. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

## What if I used a discount code on my order?

The refund will be for the discounted price you paid, not the original full price. Discount codes cannot be reused after a return.

## Can I get store credit instead of a refund?

You can request store credit during the return process. Store credit is issued immediately and never expires, so you can use it anytime.

## What if I paid with a gift card?

Gift card credits are refunded back to your account as account credit. You can use the credit for future purchases.

## How are refunds processed for different payment methods?

Refunds are credited to the original payment method. For credit cards, refunds appear as a credit on your statement. For PayPal, it's refunded to your PayPal account. Digital wallet refunds go back to the original payment source.

## What if my bank hasn't received my refund?

It can take 3-5 additional business days after we process the refund for your bank to credit it to your account. Contact your bank if it doesn't appear after 5 business days.

## Can I refuse delivery and get a refund?

Yes, you can refuse delivery of an order. Contact us before the package arrives, and we'll arrange a return. Once refused, the return is processed as normal.

## Is there a fee for requesting a refund?

No, there are no fees for refunds within our standard return window. We provide prepaid return shipping labels at no cost.

## What happens if I return a high-value order?

High-value orders are processed the same way as regular returns. We may request additional documentation to verify the return, but there are no additional fees.

## Can I get a refund for used shoes?

No, used or worn shoes do not qualify for refunds. Shoes must be in new, unworn condition with all tags and original packaging.

## What if the product quality is poor?

If you receive shoes with defects or poor quality, we'll either replace them or issue a full refund, even outside the standard return window.

## How are partial refunds handled?

If you return part of an order, the refund is calculated for only the returned items. Other items in the order are not affected.

## Can I get a refund and keep the shoes?

No, refunds are only issued after we receive and inspect your returned shoes. If you keep the shoes, you won't receive a refund.

## What if I have multiple returns pending?

Each return is processed independently. Refunds for each return are issued once that specific return is received and inspected.

## Are taxes refunded?

Yes, applicable taxes are included in your refund amount when you return shoes.

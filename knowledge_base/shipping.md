# Shipping Information

## What are the available shipping options?

We offer four shipping methods: Standard Shipping (5-7 business days), Express Shipping (2-3 business days), Overnight Shipping (1 business day), and International Shipping (10-21 business days depending on destination).

## How much does shipping cost?

Standard Shipping is free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99. Express Shipping is $12.99, Overnight Shipping is $24.99. International shipping costs vary by location and are calculated at checkout.

## Does shipping include insurance?

Standard shipments up to $100 include basic coverage. Higher-value orders and international shipments require optional shipping insurance, which you can add at checkout.

## Can I change my shipping address after placing my order?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, you can update your shipping address. Contact support as soon as possible with your order number and new address.

## What happens if I provide an incorrect address?

Contact us immediately with your order number. If the package hasn't shipped, we can correct the address. If already shipped, we'll work with the carrier to redirect it or arrange a refund.

## Do you ship to PO boxes?

No, we ship only to physical addresses for standard orders. International shipments may have additional restrictions.

## Can I ship to multiple addresses in one order?

No, each order ships to one address. To ship to multiple addresses, place separate orders.

## Do you offer free shipping?

Yes, standard shipping is free on orders over $50. We also offer free shipping on all orders during select promotional periods.

## How often do you ship orders?

Orders are typically shipped Monday through Friday. We don't ship on weekends or holidays. Orders placed on holidays are processed the next business day.

## Can I expedite my order?

Yes, we offer express and overnight shipping options. Select the faster option during checkout to receive your order more quickly.

## What is the tracking information?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a carrier tracking number. You can track your package in real-time through the carrier's website.

## How long are tracking numbers valid?

Tracking numbers are valid for 60 days after shipment. After that, you may not be able to track your package online, but your order history still shows the status.

## What if my package is lost during shipping?

If your package is marked as lost by the carrier, we'll investigate and either ship a replacement or issue a full refund. Contact us with your tracking number.

## Can I pick up my order at a store?

We currently only offer home delivery. Store pickup is not available at this time.

## Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 100 countries worldwide. International shipping takes 10-21 business days and may include customs fees. International rates are calculated at checkout.

## Are there countries you don't ship to?

We ship to most countries except those under trade restrictions. Some countries may have import duties or customs taxes that the customer must pay.

## Can I request a specific delivery date?

We can't guarantee specific delivery dates, but you can select Express or Overnight shipping to receive your order faster. The estimated delivery date is provided at checkout.

## What happens to my order during weather delays?

Shipping delays due to severe weather, natural disasters, or carrier disruptions may occur. These are beyond our control, but we'll keep you updated on your shipment status.

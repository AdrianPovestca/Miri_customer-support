# Order Management

## How do I place an order?

Browse our catalog and select the shoes you want. Choose your size and color, then click "Add to Cart." When you're ready to purchase, click the cart icon and follow the checkout process. Enter your shipping and payment information, review your order, and click "Place Order."

## Can I track my order?

Yes, once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also log into your account and view your order status in the "My Orders" section. The tracking number links directly to the carrier's website for real-time updates.

## How long does order processing take?

Most orders are processed within 24 business hours. Standard processing time is 1-2 business days before shipment. During busy periods, processing may take up to 3 business days.

## Can I modify my order after placing it?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, you may be able to modify it. Log into your account, go to "My Orders," and check if the order is still in "Processing" status. If so, you can make changes. Contact support if the option isn't available.

## Is there a minimum order amount?

No, there's no minimum order requirement. You can purchase a single pair of shoes or multiple pairs.

## Do you offer bulk orders?

Yes, we offer special pricing for bulk orders of 10 or more pairs. Contact our sales team at bulk@shoesstore.com for a quote.

## How many items can I order at once?

You can order up to 100 items per transaction. For larger orders, please contact our bulk sales team.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and digital wallets. We also offer installment payments through our financing partners.

## Is my payment information stored securely?

Yes, we use PCI-DSS compliant payment processing. Your payment information is encrypted and never stored on our servers. We use tokenization for secure recurring payments.

## Can I save my payment methods?

Yes, you can save multiple payment methods in your account settings. Your saved cards and digital wallets make future checkouts faster and easier.

## Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, we sell digital and physical gift cards in denominations from $25 to $500. Gift cards never expire and can be used for any purchase.

## Can I apply multiple discount codes to one order?

Typically, only one discount code can be applied per order. However, sale prices and promotion codes cannot be combined.

## How do I view my order history?

Log into your account and click "My Orders." You can view all past orders, their status, tracking information, and receipts.

## Can I reorder the same items?

Yes, in your order history, you can click "Reorder" next to any previous purchase to add those items to your cart.

## What if my order didn't arrive on the expected date?

Check your tracking number first to see the current status. If the delivery date has passed without update, contact our support team with your order number.

## Can I place an order without an account?

Yes, you can checkout as a guest. However, you won't be able to track your order or access your purchase history without creating an account.

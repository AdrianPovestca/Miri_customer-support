# Payment and Billing

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay. We also offer financing through Klarna and Afterpay for eligible customers.

## Is it safe to pay online?

Yes, our payment system uses industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliance. Your payment information is never stored on our servers and is processed through secure third-party providers.

## Can I save my payment information?

Yes, you can save multiple credit cards, digital wallets, and PayPal accounts in your account settings. Saved payments make checkout faster and easier.

## Do you charge a fee for using different payment methods?

No, there are no additional fees for any payment method. All payment methods are processed at the same cost.

## What is your pricing in my country?

Prices are displayed in your local currency based on your location. Currency conversion rates are determined by your payment provider. International customers may see different prices due to VAT, duties, and local taxes.

## Can I update my payment method after placing an order?

If your order hasn't been charged yet, you can update your payment method from your account. Contact support if you need help with this.

## What should I do if my payment was declined?

Check with your bank to ensure your card is not blocked. Common reasons include insufficient funds, security restrictions, or mismatched billing address. Try again or use a different payment method.

## Can I pay with multiple payment methods for one order?

No, each order must be paid with a single payment method. If you want to split payment between methods, place separate orders.

## Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we partner with Klarna and Afterpay to offer interest-free payment plans. These options are available at checkout for eligible customers.

## How do payment plans work?

With Klarna, you can split your purchase into 4 interest-free installments. With Afterpay, you pay 4 equal amounts every 2 weeks. Interest is applied if payments are missed.

## What is the minimum order amount for payment plans?

Klarna requires a minimum order of $35. Afterpay requires a minimum of $35 and maximum of $2,000 per order.

## Will payment plans affect my credit score?

Klarna and Afterpay perform soft credit checks that don't affect your score. Late payments may be reported to credit bureaus.

## How do I use a gift card as payment?

Enter your gift card code during checkout. The gift card balance is deducted from your total. If the balance doesn't cover the full order, you'll need to use another payment method for the difference.

## Can I combine gift cards with other discounts?

Yes, you can use a gift card along with discount codes or sale prices. The gift card is applied after discounts.

## What if I enter the wrong billing address?

If you entered an incorrect billing address, contact support immediately. We can correct it if your order hasn't been processed yet.

## Why was I charged twice for one order?

Double charges are rare but can happen during payment processing. Contact your bank and our support team with your order number. We'll investigate and issue a refund if needed.

## How do I see my billing history?

Log into your account and go to "Billing." You can view all charges, invoices, and payment dates for your purchases.

## Can I get an invoice for my order?

Yes, you can download an invoice from your order confirmation email or from your account in "My Orders."

## Do you charge a fee for invoices?

No, invoices are free and available for all orders.

## What if I notice an unauthorized charge?

Contact us immediately with your order number. If you didn't authorize the charge, we'll investigate and process a refund. You can also dispute the charge with your bank.

## How often are you processing payments?

Payments are processed immediately upon order confirmation. Digital payment methods are instant, while some bank transfers may take 1-2 business days.

## Can I change my billing address after checkout?

If your order hasn't shipped, you can update your billing address through your account. Contact support for immediate assistance.
